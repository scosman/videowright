Motion Engineering

Aerospace HUD on a blueprint grid. Charcoal canvas, cyan-white type, amber signal, cyan measurement. Elements measure themselves in: lines draw, corners and crosshairs lock on, callouts arrive on leader lines, numbers tick. Then the frame keeps breathing. HUD vocabulary

Corner frame and slot readouts are chrome, present from frame 1. Everything else is annotation: it arrives after the content it measures (draw, fill, annotate, breathe) and lets go on the release cue.

Scenes

The settled frame of each sample (ui-showcase with its camera at rest, cta in its calm final state): one focal point in amber, measurement in cyan, the same grid at the same origin in every frame.
