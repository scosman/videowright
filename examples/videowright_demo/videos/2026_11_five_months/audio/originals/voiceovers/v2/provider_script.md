# Provider Script

> Provider: ElevenLabs v4 (portal)
> Voice: Cosman-1 (instant voice clone). Portal settings: speed 1.00, stability 50, similarity boost 75 (as v1).
> Style: confident, warm tech explainer; conversational, natural pace. No audio tags.
> Pronunciation: "3 D", "G P U" and "U I" spelled with spaces so they are read letter by letter. "Videowright" sounds like "video write".
> Round 2, the whole narration in one take except the voice beat's two lines, which are their own takes (voice_then: Asher, ElevenLabs v2; voice_now: Cosman-1 with [factual] / [confident]). audio/assemble.mjs places them between "timed to every word." and "Both videos".
> Pause markers: between a pair's Then and Now lines, the sweep (each Then clip needs ~3s); after a pair's Now line, its Now clip's hold and the cut. The pause after "timed to every word." is where the assembly cuts in the voice beat. The tail is the CTA's end hold. All lengths can be re-tuned later from the same take.
