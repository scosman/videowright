# Provider Script

> Provider: ElevenLabs v4 (portal)
> Voice: Cosman-1 (instant voice clone). Portal settings: speed 1.00, stability 50, similarity boost 75 (same as demo_v2's take).
> Style: confident, warm tech explainer; conversational, natural pace. Tone comes from the writing, except the voice beat, which is the one place with audio tags ([warmly], [excited]): it demonstrates emotion cues.
> Pronunciation: "3 D", "G P U" and "U I" spelled with spaces so they are read letter by letter. "Videowright" sounds like "video write".
> Pause markers: sized for the planned animation. Between a pair's Then and Now lines: the sweep. After a pair's Now line: the Now clip's hold and the cut to the next pair. The long pause before the voice beat's Now line holds the Now clip, the "Sound on" chip and the Asher line (placed into this gap by the audio plan). The tail is the CTA's end hold. All pause lengths can be re-tuned later from the same take.
